# Benaiah

Benaiah is a digital mission that invites everyone to draw closer to God.
This app brings its devotionals, Bible study material, graphics and
podcasts to your phone, in English and Amharic, on Android and iPhone.

The name means "God has built". It is also the name of the brave captain
of King David's bodyguards.

## What you can do

- **Read devotionals and study material.** Topics are grouped into
  series, and each one brings together a devotional, study material and
  graphics.
- **Look up verses as you read.** Tap a Bible reference in any article to
  read the passage right there, then copy or share it.
- **Save and share graphics.** Open any graphic full screen, save it to
  your photos, or send it to a friend.
- **Listen to the podcast.** Episodes keep playing when you leave the app,
  with controls on your lock screen. Change the speed, or skip back and
  forward 10 seconds.
- **Watch the videos** that go with some articles.
- **Find things quickly.** Search series, topics and podcast episodes, and
  see everything a writer or host has worked on.
- **Make it yours.** Switch between English and Amharic, and choose a
  light or dark look, or let it follow your phone.
